Day 1 opens in

How it works · example: Animals
  1. Goal: the tallest tower. Name answers to today's category. Each one adds a floor.
  2. Each answer must be rarer than the one below it. Too common and it topples.
    Animals: Dog #1 → Horse #6 → Zebra #12
  3. Climb in small steps. Jump to a rare answer too soon and there's no room left above it. The more answers you know, the taller you build.
  4. 3 lives: a topple or running out of time (30 seconds per floor) costs one.
  5. The ruler beside the tower shows where each answer ranks: #1, the most common, at the bottom.
  6. Know one too rare for the top 100? Drop it, and Drop turns into Spire: +3 floors on top, but it ends your run, so save it for last.
  7. Out of ideas? End run keeps your score.
